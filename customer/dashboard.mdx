After your mess owner approves your membership and you save a delivery address, the Smart Mess dashboard becomes your daily command centre. It surfaces everything you need at a glance — the food being served today, your upcoming schedule, the current state of your subscription, and a real-time running bill — without requiring you to dig through menus.

## Joining Your Mess

Before you can reach the dashboard, you need to create an account and receive owner approval.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the join link or scan the QR">
    Your mess owner shares a unique invite URL in the format `/join/{token}`, or you can scan the downloadable QR code. Opening either takes you to the registration page for that specific mess.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your details">
    Fill in your full name, a 10-digit phone number (this is your login ID), and choose a password of at least 8 characters. New accounts require a phone verification step before submission.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit your request">
    Click **Join this mess**. You will see a confirmation: _"Join request sent. Log in with your phone and chosen password to check approval."_ You can log in immediately, but the workspace is locked until the owner acts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for owner approval">
    While pending you will see a **Waiting for owner approval** screen. The owner reviews join requests from their dashboard and clicks **Approve & assign plan**. You receive no external notification — check back by opening the app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your delivery address">
    Once approved, the app prompts you to save a complete delivery address (minimum 10 characters) before any other feature is accessible. Go to **Delivery**, enter your address and any drop-off instructions, and save. This unlocks the full workspace.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  A phone number identifies exactly one account across Smart Mess. If you already have an account and try to re-register with the same number, the app recognises you and updates your pending request instead of creating a duplicate.
</Note>

## What the Dashboard Shows

Once your account is active and your address is saved, the dashboard refreshes automatically when the browser tab regains focus and after any meal action (skip, undo, guest add).

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Today's Meals" icon="utensils">
    Lists every published meal for today with its menu description, meal type (breakfast / lunch / dinner), AI-generated food photo, and your current status — Confirmed, Skipped, or Guest Added. A **can\_manage** flag shows when you can still skip or add a guest before the cutoff.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Upcoming Meals" icon="calendar-days">
    Shows all published meals from today onward, sorted by date and cutoff time. Useful for planning skips or guest additions in advance.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Current Subscription" icon="id-card">
    Displays your active plan name, covered meal types, start and end dates, skip count, and any subscription extension days earned from skips.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Live Bill Summary" icon="receipt">
    A running total calculated as **Base Price − Skip Deductions + Guest & Extra Costs**. Updates immediately after every meal action without a page reload.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## In-App Notifications

Smart Mess does not send email or push notifications. All alerts are delivered inside the app.

<Info>
  The bill notification banner polls the server every **15 seconds** while you are logged in. When your owner generates a new bill snapshot, the banner appears automatically — you do not need to refresh the page.
</Info>

## Language Settings

You can switch the interface between **English** and **Hindi** at any time using the language selector in the navigation bar.

<Tip>
  Your language preference is stored in the browser under the key `mess-language` and persists across sessions on the same device. Dynamic content such as menu descriptions and offer text is translated through the backend; owner-provided Hindi descriptions take precedence over automatic translation.
</Tip>

## Frequently Asked Questions

<Accordion title="I approved my account but I can't see any meals. What should I do?">
  You must save a delivery address before the workspace is accessible. Go to **Delivery**, enter a complete address (at least 10 characters), and save. The dashboard will load your meals immediately after.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I belong to more than one mess?">
  A single phone number can be associated with multiple messes. After logging in you will see a mess-selection screen if you have memberships at more than one location. Each mess has its own separate dashboard, meals, and billing.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The dashboard shows 'Waiting for owner approval' even after I registered. How long does this take?">
  Approval is a manual step performed by the mess owner. There is no automated time limit. If you have been waiting a long time, contact your owner directly — Smart Mess does not send approval reminders.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why does my bill amount change without me doing anything?">
  The bill recalculates live whenever the owner updates guest pricing, records a payment, or generates a new bill snapshot. The 15-second notification poll and focus-based refresh keep the figure current.
</Accordion>
